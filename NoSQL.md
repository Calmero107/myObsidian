
NoSQL (short for Not Only SQL) refers to non-relational databases, mainly targeting key-value, document, and graph data storage. In addition, NoSQL databases naturally support features such as distribution, data redundancy, and data sharding, aiming to provide scalable, highly available, and high-performance data storage solutions.

A common misconception is that NoSQL databases, or non-relational databases, don’t store relational data very well. NoSQL databases can store relational data—they just do it differently than relational databases.

Representatives of NoSQL databases: HBase, Cassandra, MongoDB, Redis.
