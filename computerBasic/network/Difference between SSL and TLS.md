
**There is not much difference between SSL and TLS.**

SSL refers to the Secure Sockets Layer, which was first released in 1996. The first release of SSL was actually its 3.0 version. SSL 1.0 never came to the market, and SSL 2.0 had a major flaw (DROWN flaw - Decrypting RSA with Obsolete and Weakened eNcryption). Soon, in 1999, SSL 3.0 was further upgraded and **the new version was named TLS 1.0** . Therefore, TLS is based on SSL, but due to customary naming, the core encryption protocol in HTTPS is usually confused as SSL/TLS.
