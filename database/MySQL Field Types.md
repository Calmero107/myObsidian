
MySQL field types can be simply divided into three categories:
- **Numeric types** : integer (TINYINT, SMALLINT, MEDIUMINT, INT, and BIGINT), floating-point (FLOAT and DOUBLE), fixed-point (DECIMAL)
- **String types** : CHAR, VARCHAR, TINYTEXT, TEXT, MEDIUMTEXT, LONGTEXT, TINYBLOB, BLOB, MEDIUMBLOB, and LONGBLOB. The most commonly used are CHAR and VARCHAR.
- **Date and time types** : YEAR, TIME, DATE, DATETIME, TIMESTAMP, etc.
