
AOP (Aspect Oriented Programming) is aspect-oriented programming. AOP is a continuation of OOP (object-oriented programming). The two are complementary and not contradictory.

The purpose of AOP is to separate cross-cutting concerns (such as logging, transaction management, permission control, interface current limiting, interface idempotence, etc.) from the core business logic, and to achieve ==code reuse and decoupling==(tách rời) through dynamic proxy, bytecode manipulation and other technologies, thereby improving code maintainability and scalability.

The purpose of OOP is to encapsulate business logic according to the attributes and behaviors of objects, and to achieve ==code modularization and hierarchy== (which can also achieve code reuse) through concepts such as classes, objects, inheritance, and polymorphism, thereby improving code readability and maintainability.
